INFRA RISK RADAR — REAL-WORLD DATA/ACCURACY BUILD

This build preserves the existing Map & Conflict implementation exactly.

Accuracy policy:
- Official PAIMANA April 2026 portfolio figures are shown as official portfolio context.
- The application never fabricates project records to make a route look populated.
- Exact route-project matching requires source-backed Point/LineString/MultiLineString geometry.
- PAIMANA metadata without authoritative geometry is not treated as an exact map conflict.
- Risk scores are derived calculations from available project evidence; they are not official PAIMANA risk ratings.
- Fabricated/hardcoded ML accuracy metrics have been removed. A real ML accuracy score must be generated only after training/testing on a documented historical dataset.
- OSM construction evidence is supplementary and is not presented as official government project progress.

Official April 2026 PAIMANA snapshot used in UI:
1,981 ongoing projects; 17 Central Ministries/Departments; original cost approximately ₹37.13 lakh crore; revised cost ₹42.78 lakh crore; expenditure ₹20.36 lakh crore.
Source: PIB/MoSPI Flash Report, published 25 May 2026.
